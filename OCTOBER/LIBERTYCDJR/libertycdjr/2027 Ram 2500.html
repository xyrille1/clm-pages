The 2027 Ram 2500 Delivers Strength for Demanding Jobs

The Ram 2500 offers two powertrains to suit different heavy-duty needs. The 6.4L HEMI V8 supplies 405 horsepower and 429 lb-ft of torque for hauling supplies, transporting tools and managing worksite duties. Drivers who prioritize low-end pulling power can choose the High-Output 6.7L Cummins Turbo Diesel, which produces 430 horsepower and 1,075 lb-ft of torque. Both engines use an eight-speed automatic transmission.

Depending on configuration, the Ram 2500 can tow up to 20,000 pounds with the diesel engine or carry a payload of up to 4,580 pounds with select gasoline-powered setups. These ratings make configuration important: the truck's engine, cab, drivetrain, axle ratio and equipment all affect its final capability.
