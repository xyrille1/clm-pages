Frequently Asked Questions About the 2026 Mazda CX-50

Which 2026 CX-50 engine produces the most horsepower?

The available 2.5L turbocharged engine produces up to 256 horsepower and 320 lb-ft of torque with recommended premium fuel.

Does every 2026 Mazda CX-50 have all-wheel drive?

Yes. Gasoline models use standard i-ACTIV AWD®, while the Hybrid uses electronic all-wheel drive.

How much can the 2026 CX-50 tow?

Turbo models can tow up to 3,500 pounds when properly equipped. Non-turbo gasoline models are rated for up to 2,000 pounds, and Hybrid models for up to 1,500 pounds.

Is a hybrid version of the CX-50 available?

Yes. The 2026 CX-50 Hybrid produces 219 total system horsepower and uses electronic all-wheel drive.
