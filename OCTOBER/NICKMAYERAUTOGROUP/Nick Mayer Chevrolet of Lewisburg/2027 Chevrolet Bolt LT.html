The Electric Motor Produces 210 Horsepower

The Bolt LT uses a single permanent-magnet electric motor that produces 210 horsepower and 169 lb-ft of torque. Front-wheel drive sends that output through a single-speed gearset, while regenerative braking helps recover energy during deceleration. Chevrolet also provides three regenerative braking settings: Off, Normal, and High.

The Battery Provides 262 Miles of EPA-Estimated Range

The 65-kWh lithium iron phosphate battery gives the Bolt LT an EPA-estimated 262 miles of range on a full charge. Chevrolet rates the vehicle for up to 11.5 kW of AC charging and up to 150 kW of DC fast charging. A compatible DC fast charger can replenish the battery from 10% to 80% in approximately 25 minutes, although actual charging times vary with battery temperature, starting charge level, and charger output.

That charging capability can benefit Lewisburg-area drivers who combine weekday commuting with trips toward Columbia, Chapel Hill, Cornersville, or Interstate 65. Lewisburg's transportation network includes I-65 access at Exits 32 and 37, making charging flexibility relevant for both local and regional travel.

The Five-Passenger Cabin Combines Space and Digital Technology

The Bolt LT seats five passengers and provides a redesigned interior with an open center console, passenger-side dash storage, multiple USB-C ports, and large digital displays. Chevrolet gives the LT an 11.3-inch infotainment touchscreen and an 11-inch Driver Information Center.

The cargo area provides 16 cubic feet behind the rear seats and up to 56 cubic feet with the rear seats folded, according to Car and Driver's measurements.

The NACS Port Expands Charging Access

The 2027 Bolt uses a standard native NACS charging port, allowing compatible Tesla Superchargers to be used without an adapter. Chevrolet also supports Level 2 AC charging, while DC fast charging can reach 150 kW at compatible stations.

The infotainment system uses Google built-in rather than Apple CarPlay or Android Auto phone mirroring. Native Google applications provide navigation and entertainment functions directly through the vehicle's touchscreen.
