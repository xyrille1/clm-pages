The Acadia Lineup Covers Family, Premium and Off-Road Needs

  • Elevation: The entry trim combines the 2.5L Turbo engine with a 15-inch infotainment screen, Bose 12-speaker audio, HD Surround Vision, and available AWD.
  • AT4: Active Torque Control AWD, all-terrain tires, increased ride height, off-road suspension tuning, and Terrain Mode support more demanding driving conditions.
  • Denali: Heated and ventilated front seats, leather-appointed seating surfaces, a configurable 8-inch Head-Up Display, 20-inch wheels, and additional premium equipment define the trim.
  • Denali Ultimate: Super Cruise, massaging heated and ventilated front seats, a panoramic sunroof, and a Bose 16-speaker Performance Series system add higher-level equipment.
