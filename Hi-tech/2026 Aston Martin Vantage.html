2026 Aston Martin Vantage Brings Twin-Turbo V8 Performance

The 2026 Vantage uses a 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 producing 656 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque. An eight-speed automatic transmission sends the engine's output to the rear wheels, while an electronically controlled limited-slip differential manages torque across the rear axle.

The Vantage S adds 14 horsepower, bringing total output to 670 horsepower. Aston Martin also gives the S revised chassis components, adaptive damper tuning, a stiffer rear subframe, and additional changes intended to increase driver feedback.

FAQs

What engine does the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage have?

The 2026 Vantage uses a twin-turbocharged 4.0-liter V8 engine producing 656 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque in standard form.

How much horsepower does the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage produce?

The standard Vantage produces 656 horsepower, while the Vantage S increases output to 670 horsepower.

Is the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage rear-wheel drive?

Yes. The 2026 Vantage sends its power to the rear wheels through an eight-speed automatic transmission.

What transmission does the 2026 Vantage use?

The Vantage uses an eight-speed automatic transmission. There is no manual transmission offered for the 2026 model.

How fast is the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage?

Aston Martin lists a top speed of 202 mph for the Vantage. Car and Driver recorded a 3.2-second 0-to-60-mph time with the standard Vantage during its testing.

Does Aston Martin offer a Vantage Roadster for 2026?

Yes. The 2026 model year introduces the Vantage Roadster, which uses a 656-horsepower V8 and a Z-fold fabric roof that opens or closes in approximately 6.8 seconds.

How much horsepower does the Vantage S have?

The 2026 Vantage S produces 670 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque.

Can the 2026 Vantage be customized?

Yes. Aston Martin offers extensive specification choices covering exterior colors, wheels, brake calipers, interior materials, trim, stitching, and other details, with additional personalization available through Q by Aston Martin.
