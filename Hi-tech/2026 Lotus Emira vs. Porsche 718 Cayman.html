Two Mid-Engine Sports Cars With Distinct Powertrain Choices

The 2026 Emira gives drivers multiple ways to configure its mid-engine platform. The Turbo uses a Mercedes-AMG-sourced 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder, producing 360 horsepower, while the Turbo SE raises output to 400 horsepower and 354 lb-ft of torque. The V6 SE uses a supercharged 3.5-liter V6 producing 400 horsepower and 310 lb-ft.

The latest gasoline 718 Cayman specification published by Porsche USA uses a 2.0-liter turbocharged boxer four-cylinder producing 300 horsepower and 280 lb-ft. Porsche pairs it with either a six-speed manual or available seven-speed PDK.

  • Emira Powertrain Details Include

    • 2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder
    • 360 hp in Turbo configuration
    • 400 hp in Turbo SE
    • 354 lb-ft in Turbo SE
    • 3.5L supercharged V6 in V6 SE
    • Rear-wheel drive
    • 8-speed DCT with Turbo models

  • 718 Cayman Powertrain Details Include

    • 2.0L turbocharged boxer four-cylinder
    • 300 horsepower
    • 280 lb-ft of torque
    • Six-speed manual transmission
    • Available seven-speed PDK
    • Mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout

Performance Efficiency Depends on the Configuration

The Emira's fuel economy varies according to its powertrain and transmission. MotorTrend reports the V6 automatic at an expected 18 mpg city and 26 mpg highway, while the latest gasoline 718 Cayman is rated at 21 mpg city and 27 mpg highway according to current comparison data.

  • Emira Efficiency Facts

    • V6 automatic estimated at 18 mpg city
    • V6 automatic estimated at 26 mpg highway

  • 718 Cayman Efficiency Facts

    • 21 mpg city

    • 24 mpg combined
    • 14.2-gallon fuel tank

Chassis Engineering Keeps the Driver Central

Both vehicles use a mid-engine configuration and rear-wheel drive, but their chassis philosophies have distinct characteristics. Lotus equips the Emira with electrohydraulic steering and double-wishbone suspension, while the Cayman uses Porsche's mid-engine platform and carefully calibrated electromechanical steering.

  • Emira Chassis Features

    • Mid-engine architecture
    • Rear-wheel drive
    • Electrohydraulic power steering
    • Double-wishbone suspension
    • Tour and Sport drive modes
    • Available Driver's Pack

  • 718 Cayman Chassis Features

    • Mid-engine architecture
    • Rear-wheel drive
    • Electromechanical power steering
    • Six-speed manual availability
    • Available Porsche Active Suspension Management
    • Performance-focused braking system
