The 2026 Aston Martin Vantage and 2026 Porsche 911 Carrera approach the luxury sports coupe from different engineering philosophies. The Vantage uses a front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout with a twin-turbocharged V8, while the 911 Carrera places its twin-turbo flat-six behind the rear axle and sends power to the rear wheels. Both pair their engines with quick-shifting automatic transmissions and provide a focused two-seat coupe configuration.

Power Comes From Two Very Different Engine Layouts

The Vantage places its 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 at the front and routes 656 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque through an 8-speed automatic transmission to the rear wheels. Its electronically controlled limited-slip differential and adaptive chassis systems support the car's rear-drive performance character.

The 911 Carrera uses a 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged boxer six-cylinder engine mounted at the rear. It produces 388 horsepower and 331 lb-ft of torque, with an 8-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung transmission managing gear changes.

Fuel Economy Reflects Their Performance-Oriented Engineering

Neither coupe is designed around maximum fuel economy, but both provide highway-oriented efficiency figures for their respective performance classes. The 2026 Vantage is EPA-rated at 15 mpg city, 22 mpg highway, and 18 mpg combined. Edmunds reports 21 mpg combined for the base 2026 911 Carrera coupe.

Chassis Design Shapes the Driving Experience

The Vantage combines its front-engine layout with rear-wheel drive, adaptive dampers, revised chassis tuning, and performance-focused tires. The 911 Carrera takes a different route with its rear-engine architecture, Porsche Active Suspension Management, electromechanical steering, and rear-wheel drive.

  • Vantage Chassis Features

    • Revised chassis tuning
    • Performance-oriented Michelin tires

  • 911 Carrera Chassis Features

    • Rear-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout
    • Porsche Active Suspension Management
    • Electromechanical power steering
    • 19-inch front and 20-inch rear Carrera wheels
    • Performance-focused braking system

FAQs

Is the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage more powerful than the 2026 Porsche 911 Carrera?

Yes. The Vantage produces 656 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque, while the 911 Carrera produces 388 horsepower and 331 lb-ft.

Does the 2026 Vantage have rear-wheel drive?

Yes. The standard 2026 Vantage uses a front-engine, rear-wheel-drive configuration.

Does the 2026 Porsche 911 Carrera have rear-wheel drive?

Yes. The standard Carrera uses rear-wheel drive. Porsche also offers other 911 variants with different drivetrain configurations.

What transmission does the 2026 Vantage use?

The 2026 Vantage uses an 8-speed automatic transmission.

What transmission does the 2026 Porsche 911 Carrera use?

The standard 2026 Carrera uses an 8-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung, or PDK, automatic transmission.

How fast is the 2026 Aston Martin Vantage?

The Vantage has a claimed 0-60 mph time of 3.5 seconds and a top speed of 202 mph.

How fast is the 2026 Porsche 911 Carrera?

The 2026 Carrera has a manufacturer-listed 0-60 mph time of 3.9 seconds and a top track speed of 183 mph. With the available Sport Chrono Package, Porsche lists 3.7 seconds to 60 mph.
