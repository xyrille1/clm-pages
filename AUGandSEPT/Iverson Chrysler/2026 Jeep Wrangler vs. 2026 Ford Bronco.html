The 2026 Jeep Wrangler and 2026 Ford Bronco are designed for drivers who want more than conventional SUV transportation. 2026 Jeep Wrangler Delivers Multiple Powertrain Choices

The 2026 Wrangler gives Mitchell drivers several ways to configure its powertrain. The 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 produces 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque, while the available 2.0-liter turbocharged engine develops 270 horsepower and 295 lb-ft.

Wrangler performance highlights

  • 3.6L Pentastar V6 produces 285 hp and 260 lb-ft.
  • Available 2.0L turbocharged engine develops 270 hp and 295 lb-ft.
  • 6.4L V8 produces 470 hp and 470 lb-ft in the Wrangler 392.
  • Four-wheel drive is standard.
  • Properly equipped four-door models can tow up to 5,000 pounds.
  • Rock-Trac and Command-Trac 4x4 systems are available.

Bronco performance highlights

  • 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 produces 300 hp and 325 lb-ft on premium fuel.
  • Available 2.7L EcoBoost V6 produces 330 hp and 415 lb-ft on premium fuel.
  • 3.0L EcoBoost V6 produces 418 hp and 440 lb-ft in the Raptor.
  • Most configurations can tow up to 3,500 pounds.
  • Raptor increases maximum towing capacity to 4,500 pounds.
  • Available G.O.A.T. Modes adjust vehicle systems for different terrain conditions.

2026 Jeep Wrangler Supports Outdoor Utility

The Wrangler's two-door and four-door configurations let buyers prioritize compact trail dimensions or additional passenger and cargo room. Four-door versions can accommodate up to five occupants and provide up to 72.4 cubic feet of cargo space with the rear seating folded.

Wrangler utility highlights

  • Two-door and four-door configurations are available.
  • Four-door versions seat up to five people.
  • Maximum cargo capacity reaches 72.4 cubic feet.
  • Removable doors support open-air driving.
  • Removable roof panels provide additional configuration flexibility.
  • Folding rear seats expand the cargo area.

Bronco utility highlights

  • Two-door versions seat four, while four-door versions seat five.
  • Four-door hardtop models provide up to 77.6 cubic feet of cargo space.
  • Four-door soft-top models provide up to 83.0 cubic feet.
  • Two-door versions provide up to 52.0 cubic feet with the rear seats folded.
  • Removable doors and roof configurations are available.

2026 Jeep Wrangler Connects Trail Capability With Technology

The Wrangler places modern connectivity inside a cabin designed around its traditional off-road layout. A 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen provides access to vehicle and media functions, while wireless smartphone integration supports compatible Apple CarPlay and Android Auto devices.

Wrangler technology highlights

  • 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen is standard.
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay compatibility is available.
  • Wireless Android Auto compatibility is available.
  • Available navigation provides connected route guidance.
  • Available driver-assistance features add support for everyday driving.
  • Trail-focused technology works with the Wrangler's four-wheel-drive systems.

Bronco technology highlights

  • 12-inch SYNC 4 touchscreen is standard.
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility are included.
  • Available 360-Degree Camera provides additional exterior visibility.
  • Ford Connectivity Package provides connected services and Wi-Fi capability.
  • G.O.A.T. Modes coordinate vehicle settings for different driving conditions.
