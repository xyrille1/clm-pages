2027 Ford Expedition in Buda, TX

The 2027 Ford Expedition is a full-size, body-on-frame SUV designed for families, professionals, and active drivers who need three-row seating, substantial towing capability, and configurable cargo space. Ford builds the Expedition on a high-strength steel frame with independent rear suspension, offering a balance of passenger comfort and heavy-duty hauling performance.

2027 Ford Expedition Key Highlights

The 2027 Ford Expedition uses a 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine as its standard powertrain.

The 3.5L EcoBoost V6 produces 400 horsepower and 480 lb-ft of torque.

The available High-Output 3.5L EcoBoost V6 produces 440 horsepower and 510 lb-ft of torque.

Ford pairs the EcoBoost engine with a 10-speed automatic transmission.

2027 Ford Expedition Creates Flexible Passenger and Cargo Space

The 2027 Ford Expedition seats up to eight passengers across three rows. Second-row captain's chairs are available for seven-passenger seating with easier third-row access. The Expedition provides approximately 19.3 cubic feet of cargo space behind the third row, approximately 63.6 cubic feet behind the second row, and approximately 104.6 cubic feet behind the first row with all rear seats folded. Power-folding second- and third-row seats allow drivers to reconfigure the interior between passenger and cargo use.

2027 Ford Expedition Supports Towing for Buda and Central Texas Adventures

The 2027 Ford Expedition is built on a high-strength steel frame designed for towing boats, trailers, campers, and recreational equipment. A properly equipped Expedition can tow up to 9,600 pounds. 2027 Ford Expedition Helps Support Driver and Passenger Safety

Ford equips the Expedition with driver-assistance technology intended to provide additional support for family and highway driving.
