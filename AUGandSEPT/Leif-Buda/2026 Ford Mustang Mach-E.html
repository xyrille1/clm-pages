2026 Ford Mustang Mach-E Delivers Electric Performance

The 2026 Mustang Mach-E uses a single rear electric motor in RWD configurations or dual electric motors for eAWD models. Select and Premium models can use the 73-kWh standard-range battery, while available extended-range batteries provide additional driving range. Ford rates the standard-range RWD configuration at 264 horsepower and 387 lb-ft of torque, while standard-range eAWD produces 325 horsepower and 500 lb-ft. Extended-range eAWD Select and Premium models produce 370 horsepower and 500 lb-ft.

2026 Ford Mustang Mach-E Provides Up to 320 Miles of EPA-Estimated Range

Battery and drivetrain determine the Mach-E's EPA-estimated range. Select and Premium models can reach 260 miles with the standard-range battery and RWD, while eAWD models reach 240 miles with the standard-range battery. The Premium RWD with the available extended-range battery reaches 320 miles, while extended-range eAWD Select and Premium models reach 300 miles. The GT reaches 280 miles, and the Mustang Mach-E Rally reaches 255 miles.

Ford also offers home and public charging solutions, with NACS compatibility available through a fast-charging adapter. Standard-range RWD models require approximately 7.8 hours for a full charge using a 48-amp charger and 240-volt outlet under Ford's stated conditions.

2026 Ford Mustang Mach-E Combines Five-Passenger Seating With Flexible Cargo Space

The Mach-E combines an SUV-style cabin with a five-seat layout and flexible cargo area. Select and Premium models provide 43.3 inches of front legroom and 38.1 inches of rear legroom. Cargo capacity measures 29.7 cubic feet behind the rear seats, while maximum cargo capacity varies by trim, reaching 59.7 cubic feet in the Select. A 2.6-cubic-foot front trunk is also available across the lineup.
