2026 Ford Explorer Delivers Turbocharged Performance

The 2026 Ford Explorer gives Buda-area drivers two EcoBoost engine choices. The 2.3-liter four-cylinder produces 300 horsepower and 310 lb-ft of torque, while the available 3.0-liter twin-turbo V6 produces 400 horsepower and 415 lb-ft. A 10-speed automatic transmission is standard, with rear-wheel drive or available Intelligent 4WD depending on configuration.

2026 Ford Explorer Provides Key Performance Features

  • 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 produces 300 hp and 310 lb-ft.
  • 3.0L EcoBoost V6 produces 400 hp and 415 lb-ft.
  • 10-speed automatic transmission is standard.
  • Rear-wheel drive is standard on applicable 2.3L configurations.
  • Intelligent 4WD is available on select configurations.
  • Tremor includes Intelligent 4WD as standard.
  • Maximum towing capacity reaches 5,000 pounds when properly equipped.

2026 Honda Pilot Performance Features

  • 3.5L V6 produces 285 hp and 262 lb-ft.
  • 10-speed automatic transmission is standard.
  • Front-wheel drive is standard on applicable configurations.
  • i-VTM4 AWD is available or standard depending on trim.
  • TrailSport includes an off-road-tuned suspension and all-terrain tires.
  • AWD configurations can tow up to 5,000 pounds.
  • Seven drive modes are available on applicable AWD configurations.
