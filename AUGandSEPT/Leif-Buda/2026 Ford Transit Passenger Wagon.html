2026 Ford Transit Passenger Wagon in Buda, TX

The 2026 Ford Transit Passenger Wagon is a full-size people mover built for organizations, families, shuttle operators, and businesses that need substantial passenger capacity without moving into a bus chassis. Ford offers the passenger van with seating for up to 15 people, two gasoline V6 engines, rear-wheel drive, available Intelligent All-Wheel Drive, and multiple roof configurations.

2026 Ford Transit Passenger Wagon Uses Two V6 Powertrains

Ford gives the passenger wagon two gasoline V6 choices. The standard 3.5L PFDI V6 produces 275 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. Buyers who need additional output can choose the 3.5L EcoBoost® V6, which produces 300 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque. Both engines work with a 10-speed automatic transmission.

Frequently Asked Questions About the 2026 Ford Transit Passenger Wagon

The Transit Passenger Wagon can be configured to seat up to 15 passengers.

The standard engine is a 3.5L PFDI V6 producing 275 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. A 3.5L EcoBoost® V6 producing 300 horsepower and 400 lb-ft is available.

Rear-wheel drive is standard, while Intelligent All-Wheel Drive is available on gas-powered configurations.

Ford lists maximum towing capacity of up to 4,500 pounds when properly configured. Actual capacity depends on the vehicle configuration and equipment.

The 2026 Transit receives a standard 12-inch center display with SYNC® 4, along with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™.
