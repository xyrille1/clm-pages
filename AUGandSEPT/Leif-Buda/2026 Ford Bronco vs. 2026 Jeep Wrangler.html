2026 Ford Bronco Delivers Turbocharged Performance

The 2026 Bronco gives Buda-area drivers multiple powertrain choices for daily commuting, highway travel, and off-road recreation. The 2.3-liter EcoBoost produces 300 horsepower and 325 lb-ft of torque, while the 2.7-liter EcoBoost V6 produces 330 horsepower and 415 lb-ft. The Bronco Raptor increases output to 418 horsepower and 440 lb-ft.

2026 Ford Bronco Provides Key Performance Features

  • 2.3L EcoBoost produces 300 hp and 325 lb-ft.
  • 2.7L EcoBoost V6 produces 330 hp and 415 lb-ft.
  • Bronco Raptor produces 418 hp and 440 lb-ft.
  • 4x4 capability is standard across the Bronco lineup.
  • A seven-speed manual or 10-speed automatic is available depending on configuration.
  • G.O.A.T. Modes provide selectable settings for different terrain conditions.

2026 Jeep Wrangler Provides Key Performance Features

  • 3.6L Pentastar V6 produces 285 hp and 260 lb-ft.
  • 2.0L Turbo I-4 produces 270 hp and 295 lb-ft.
  • MOAB 392 uses a 6.4L HEMI V8 producing 470 hp and 470 lb-ft.
  • 4x4 capability is built into the Wrangler lineup.
  • Select four-door models can tow up to 5,000 pounds.
  • Rubicon models can include an electronic front sway bar disconnect.
