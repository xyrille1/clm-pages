2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre Delivers Silent Electric Performance

The 2026 Spectre uses two electric motors to produce 577 horsepower and 664 lb-ft of torque in standard configuration. All-wheel drive distributes the available power across all four wheels, giving the large coupe immediate electric acceleration without the sound and shifting associated with a conventional combustion engine.

Black Badge Spectre raises output to 650 horsepower and 793 lb-ft of torque. The additional output accompanies revisions to the suspension and steering intended to give the Black Badge version a more responsive character while retaining the quiet, composed approach associated with the Spectre.

FAQs

Is the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre electric?

Yes. The 2026 Spectre uses a fully electric dual-motor powertrain and all-wheel drive.

How much horsepower does the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre have?

The standard Spectre produces 577 horsepower. Black Badge Spectre increases output to 650 horsepower.

How much torque does the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre produce?

The standard Spectre produces 664 lb-ft of torque, while Black Badge Spectre produces 793 lb-ft.

How far can the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre travel on a charge?

The 2026 Spectre has an EPA-estimated range of up to 277 miles when equipped with 22-inch wheels.

How many people can the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre seat?

The Spectre is a four-seat coupe with two front seats and two rear seats.

Does the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre have all-wheel drive?

Yes. All Spectre models use an all-wheel-drive electric powertrain.

How fast is the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre?

Car and Driver recorded a 3.7-second 0-to-60-mph time during testing of the Spectre.

What is the Black Badge Spectre?

The Black Badge Spectre is the higher-output version of the coupe. It produces 650 horsepower and 793 lb-ft of torque and receives suspension, steering, and exterior changes that distinguish it from the standard Spectre.

Can the 2026 Rolls-Royce Spectre be customized?

Yes. Rolls-Royce offers extensive Bespoke personalization covering exterior colors, coachlines, wheels, interior leather, veneers, stitching, illuminated details, headliners, treadplates, and other specification elements.
