2026 Lotus Emira Offers Two Distinct Powertrains

The Emira Turbo uses a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder producing 360 horsepower, while Turbo SE increases output from the same basic engine to 400 horsepower. Both versions use an eight-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission.

The V6 SE takes a different approach with a supercharged 3.5-liter V6 generating 400 horsepower. Buyers can choose between a six-speed manual and six-speed automatic, giving the V6 SE a more traditional sports-car configuration.

How much horsepower does the 2026 Lotus Emira have?

The Turbo produces 360 horsepower, while Turbo SE and V6 SE models produce 400 horsepower.

Does the 2026 Lotus Emira have a manual transmission?

Yes. The V6 SE is available with a six-speed manual transmission.

What engine does the Emira V6 SE use?

The V6 SE uses a supercharged 3.5-liter V6 producing 400 horsepower.

What transmission does the Emira Turbo use?

The Turbo uses an eight-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission.

How many people does the 2026 Lotus Emira seat?

The Emira is a two-seat sports car.

Does the Emira have Apple CarPlay?
