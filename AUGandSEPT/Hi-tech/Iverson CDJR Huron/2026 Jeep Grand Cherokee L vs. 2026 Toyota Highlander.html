Huron-area SUV shoppers comparing three-row models can look at the 2026 Jeep Grand Cherokee L and 2026 Toyota Highlander for different approaches to family transportation. 2026 Jeep Grand Cherokee L Offers V6 and Turbocharged Four-Cylinder Power

The 2026 Grand Cherokee L gives Huron drivers a choice between the 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 and a new 2.0-liter Hurricane turbocharged four-cylinder. The V6 produces 293 horsepower, while the turbo-four increases output to 324 horsepower and 332 lb-ft of torque. Both engines use an eight-speed automatic transmission.

Grand Cherokee L Powertrain Details Include

  • 3.6L Pentastar V6 with 293 hp
  • Available 2.0L Hurricane turbo-four with 324 hp
  • Up to 332 lb-ft of torque
  • 8-speed automatic transmission
  • Rear-wheel drive or available 4WD
  • Up to 6,200 lbs. of towing capacity

Highlander Powertrain Details Include

  • 2.4L turbocharged four-cylinder
  • 265 hp and 310 lb-ft of torque
  • 8-speed automatic transmission
  • Standard all-wheel drive
  • Up to 5,000 lbs. of towing capacity
  • Available Highlander Hybrid models
