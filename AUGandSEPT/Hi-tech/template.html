2026 Lotus Emira Offers Two Distinct Powertrains

The Emira Turbo uses a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder producing 360 horsepower, while Turbo SE increases output from the same basic engine to 400 horsepower. Both versions use an eight-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission.

The V6 SE takes a different approach with a supercharged 3.5-liter V6 generating 400 horsepower. Buyers can choose between a six-speed manual and six-speed automatic, giving the V6 SE a more traditional sports-car configuration.
