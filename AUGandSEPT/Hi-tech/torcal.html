New Bentley Torcal Coming to Bentley Austin

The new Bentley Torcal introduces Bentley's first all-electric production model, combining electric performance, handcrafted luxury, intelligent technology and extensive personalization. Designed for clients seeking an ultra-luxury electric SUV, the Torcal brings Bentley craftsmanship into a new era of electric mobility.

For Austin-area buyers considering a future Bentley EV, Bentley Austin provides a local point of contact for information about the Torcal, specifications, personalization and future availability.

Bentley Torcal Delivers Electric Performance

The Torcal combines a dual-motor electric powertrain with all-wheel drive and sophisticated chassis technology. Bentley describes the Torcal as a luxury SUV designed around both performance and the quality of the traveling experience.

Key performance highlights include:

  • Up to 876 bhp and 996 lb-ft of torque with Launch Control
  • All-electric powertrain with all-wheel drive
  • Performance-focused Torcal S model
  • Four-wheel steering and advanced suspension technology
  • High-speed DC charging capability

Bentley states that the Torcal S produces 876 bhp and 1,350 Nm of torque with Launch Control, making it the most powerful production Bentley ever built.

Bentley Torcal Technology Enhances Ownership

The Torcal brings connected technology into the ownership experience rather than limiting it to the dashboard.

Owners can benefit from features including:

  • Plug and Charge capability in selected markets

  • Remote cabin pre-conditioning

Bentley Torcal FAQ

Is the Bentley Torcal an electric SUV?

Yes. The Torcal is Bentley's all-electric luxury SUV and is offered in both Torcal and performance-focused Torcal S configurations.

How powerful is the Bentley Torcal?

The Torcal S produces up to 876 bhp and 1,350 Nm of torque when Launch Control is active. Bentley identifies it as the most powerful production Bentley ever built.

Does the Bentley Torcal have all-wheel drive?

Yes. The Torcal uses an all-electric powertrain with all-wheel drive and four-wheel steering.

Can the Bentley Torcal be customized?

Yes. Bentley offers extensive personalization, including exterior finishes, wheels, interior materials, veneers and Mulliner personal commissioning.
