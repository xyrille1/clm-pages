The standard engine is the 2.7L EcoBoost V6 with 325 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque.

Yes. The XLT offers available 4x4 capability.

SuperCab models use a 6.5-foot bed, while SuperCrew models offer 5.5-foot or 6.5-foot beds.

Yes. Pro Power Onboard is available, with the PowerBoost hybrid offering up to 7.2 kW.
