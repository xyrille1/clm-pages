2027 Ford Expedition Trims Provide Different Equipment Levels

Ford offers seven 2027 Expedition configurations:

  • Active:

    Provides seating for up to eight, a 400-hp 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6, 24-inch panoramic display, 13.2-inch center display, and flexible three-row cargo space.

  • MAX Active:

    Adds the longer MAX body with up to 123.1 cubic feet of cargo capacity while retaining the Active model's 400-hp EcoBoost V6 and available 4x4.

  • Platinum:

    Adds leather-trimmed seating, heated and ventilated front captain's chairs, standard 20-inch wheels, and availability of the 440-hp High-Output V6.

  • MAX Platinum:

    Combines Platinum equipment with the longer MAX body and its expanded 123.1-cubic-foot maximum cargo capacity.

  • Tremor:

    Provides standard 4x4, the 440-hp High-Output V6, an electronic-locking differential, and off-road-focused equipment.

  • King Ranch:

    Adds Del Rio full-leather seating, heated and ventilated front seats, heated second-row captain's chairs, and distinctive King Ranch design elements.

  • MAX King Ranch:

    Combines King Ranch equipment with the extended MAX body, providing up to 123.1 cubic feet of maximum cargo capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions About the 2027 Ford Expedition

How much horsepower does the 2027 Ford Expedition have?

The standard 3.5L EcoBoost V6 produces 400 horsepower. The available High-Output version produces 440 horsepower.

How much can the 2027 Ford Expedition tow?

Properly equipped 4x4 Expedition models can tow up to 9,600 pounds with the Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow Package.

How many people can the 2027 Expedition seat?

Depending on trim and seating configuration, the Expedition can accommodate seven or eight passengers.

Does the 2027 Ford Expedition have 4x4?

Yes. 4x4 is available across the lineup, with standard 4x4 on Tremor.

Is there a longer 2027 Ford Expedition?

Yes. The Expedition MAX uses a longer body and provides up to 123.1 cubic feet of cargo space.
