2026 Ford Ranger Trims Provide Different Equipment Levels

Ford offers four 2026 Ranger trims: XL, XLT, Lariat, and Raptor.

XL

Standard 2.3L EcoBoost with a 10-speed automatic transmission, spacious SuperCrew cab, and everyday practical capability with standard 4x2 and available 4x4.

XLT

Adds upgraded exterior styling with the Sport Appearance Package, available 2.7L EcoBoost V6, and additional towing and technology equipment for daily driving.

Lariat

Includes premium interior materials like ActiveX-trimmed heated front seats, a 12-inch center display, and enhanced driver-assistance amenities for a more refined driving experience.

Raptor

Features a specialized 405-horsepower 3.0L EcoBoost V6, FOX Live Valve shocks, Watts-link rear suspension, and unique drive modes for improved capability on challenging terrain.
