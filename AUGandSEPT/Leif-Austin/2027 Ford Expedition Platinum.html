2027 Ford Expedition Platinum Delivers Full-Size Performance

The 2027 Expedition Platinum uses a 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6 producing 400 horsepower and 480 lb-ft of torque. An available High-Output version raises output to 440 horsepower and 510 lb-ft. Both engines use a 10-speed automatic transmission, while maximum towing reaches 9,600 pounds when properly equipped with 4x4 and the Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow Package.

2027 Ford Expedition Platinum Provides Flexible Passenger And Cargo Space

The Expedition Platinum seats up to eight depending on configuration and provides three rows for family transportation. Ford lists 22.9 cubic feet behind the third row, 69.9 cubic feet behind the second row, and up to 108.5 cubic feet behind the first row. The 40/20/40 PowerFold third row can also fold flat to expand usable cargo space.
