2026 Ford Bronco Sport Gives Austin Drivers Two Turbocharged Engines

The 2026 Bronco Sport offers two turbocharged powertrains. Big Bend®, Heritage, and Outer Banks® models use a 1.5L EcoBoost® three-cylinder producing 180 horsepower and 200 lb-ft of torque. The Badlands® uses a 2.0L EcoBoost® four-cylinder producing 250 horsepower and 280 lb-ft of torque.

Both engines use an eight-speed automatic transmission. The 1.5L configuration uses standard 4x4, while the 2.0L engine pairs with advanced 4x4 and a twin-clutch rear drive unit.
