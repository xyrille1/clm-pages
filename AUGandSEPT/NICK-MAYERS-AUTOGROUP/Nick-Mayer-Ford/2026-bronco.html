2026 Ford Bronco Uses G.O.A.T. Modes for Terrain Control

Ford equips the Bronco with a Terrain Management System using G.O.A.T. Modes to adjust vehicle behavior for different driving conditions. Depending on configuration, available modes include Normal, Eco, Sport, Slippery and Sand, with additional settings offered on more specialized Bronco models.

The available Sasquatch Package adds 35-inch tires, electronic-locking front and rear axles, and Bilstein position-sensitive dampers.

2026 Ford Bronco Creates Removable Open-Air Capability

The Bronco supports open-air driving with removable doors and roof components. Ford offers two-door and four-door body styles, while roof choices vary by configuration. The four-door Bronco also provides up to 83.0 cubic feet of cargo space with the rear seats folded.

Inside, a 12.0-inch touchscreen and 12.0-inch digital instrument display provide the primary technology interfaces. Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are also included.

2026 Ford Bronco Trims Provide Different Off-Road Equipment

  • Base: Establishes the lineup with 4x4, the 2.3L EcoBoost, two- or four-door availability, and essential trail equipment.
  • Big Bend: Adds equipment for everyday adventure while using the four-door body style.
  • Outer Banks: Adds comfort and convenience equipment to the four-door Bronco.
  • Badlands: Adds more specialized off-road hardware, including advanced 4x4 equipment and trail-focused suspension.
  • Heritage Edition: Combines four-door practicality with heritage-inspired styling and Sasquatch equipment.
  • Stroppe Edition: Adds desert-focused equipment and a 2.7L EcoBoost V6.
  • Raptor: Uses a 418-hp 3.0L EcoBoost V6, HOSS 4.0 suspension, FOX Live Valve dampers, and 37-inch tires.
