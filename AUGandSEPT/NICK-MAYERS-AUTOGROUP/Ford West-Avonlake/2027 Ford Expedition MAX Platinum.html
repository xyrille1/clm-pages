2027 Ford Expedition MAX Platinum Produces Turbocharged V6 Power

Ford equips the MAX Platinum with a 3.5L EcoBoost V6 producing 400 horsepower and 480 lb-ft of torque. Buyers can select the available 3.5L EcoBoost V6 High-Output engine, which increases output to 440 horsepower and 510 lb-ft of torque. Both engines pair with a 10-speed SelectShift automatic transmission.

The MAX Platinum is available with 4x2 or 4x4. Ford rates the 4x4 model for up to 9,000 pounds of towing when properly equipped with the Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow Package. The 4x2 model has a maximum towing rating of 6,300 pounds.
