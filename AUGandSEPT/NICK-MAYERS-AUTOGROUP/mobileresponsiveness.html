2026 Subaru Outback Premium Uses a 2.5-Liter BOXER Engine

The 2026 Subaru Outback Premium uses a 2.5-liter horizontally opposed SUBARU BOXER four-cylinder engine producing 180 horsepower at 5,800 rpm and 178 lb-ft of torque at 4,400 rpm. A Lineartronic CVT with an 8-speed manual mode sends power to the standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system. The 2026 Subaru Outback Premium is rated at an EPA-estimated 25 MPG city and 31 MPG highway and can tow up to 2,700 pounds.
