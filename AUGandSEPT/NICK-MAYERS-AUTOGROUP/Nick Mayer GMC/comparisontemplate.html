2026 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Delivers Four Engine Choices

The Silverado 1500 gives Dickson drivers four primary powertrain choices. The TurboMax produces 310 horsepower and 430 lb-ft of torque with an eight-speed automatic transmission. The available 5.3L V8 produces 355 horsepower and 383 lb-ft, while the 6.2L V8 produces 420 horsepower and 460 lb-ft. The 3.0L Duramax turbo-diesel produces 305 horsepower and 495 lb-ft and uses a 10-speed automatic transmission. Chevrolet lists maximum available towing of 13,300 pounds.
