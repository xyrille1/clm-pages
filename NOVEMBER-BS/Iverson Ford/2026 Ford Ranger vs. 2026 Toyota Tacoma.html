2026 Ford Ranger Performance and Capability

The 2026 Ford Ranger comes standard with a 2.3-liter EcoBoost turbocharged four-cylinder engine producing 270 horsepower and 310 lb-ft of torque. The available 2.7-liter EcoBoost V6 increases output to 315 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque. The Ranger Raptor uses a 3.0-liter EcoBoost V6 producing 405 horsepower and 430 lb-ft of torque.

2026 Ford Ranger Performance Highlights

  • 2.3L EcoBoost engine with 270 horsepower and 310 lb-ft of torque
  • Available 2.7L EcoBoost V6 with 315 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque
  • Ranger Raptor 3.0L EcoBoost V6 with 405 horsepower and 430 lb-ft of torque
  • Ten-speed automatic transmission
  • Available 4x4 on XL, XLT, and Lariat; standard on Raptor
  • Maximum towing capacity of up to 7,500 pounds on eligible trims with the required Trailer Tow Package
  • Maximum towing capacity of 5,510 pounds for Ranger Raptor

2026 Toyota Tacoma Performance Highlights

  • 2.4L i-FORCE turbocharged four-cylinder engine
  • Up to 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque with the standard i-FORCE powertrain; output varies by configuration
  • Available i-FORCE MAX hybrid system producing 326 horsepower and 465 lb-ft of torque
  • Eight-speed automatic transmission
  • Available six-speed manual transmission on select configurations
  • Available on-demand or full-time four-wheel drive, depending on trim
  • Maximum towing capacity of up to 6,500 pounds when properly equipped

Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower does the 2026 Ford Ranger produce?

The standard 2.3L EcoBoost produces 270 horsepower. The available 2.7L V6 produces 315 horsepower, while the Ranger Raptor's 3.0L V6 produces 405 horsepower.

Which truck has the higher maximum towing capacity?

The Ranger can tow up to 7,500 pounds on eligible non-Raptor trims when properly equipped. The Tacoma's maximum towing capacity is 6,500 pounds.

Does the 2026 Ford Ranger offer four-wheel drive?

Yes. Four-wheel drive is available on the XL, XLT, and Lariat trims and standard on the Ranger Raptor.
