Compare the 2027 Buick Enclave Trims

Buick offers three Enclave trims: Preferred, Sport Touring and Avenir. Each uses the same turbocharged engine, so the main differences involve styling, interior appointments and available equipment.

Preferred

The Preferred provides the core Enclave experience, including the 30-inch display, Google built-in, heated front seats and Bose Premium audio. Its equipment suits drivers looking for three-row practicality with connected technology and comfort features.

Sport Touring

The Sport Touring introduces a more distinctive appearance with high-gloss black exterior accents, a sport-oriented grille and 20-inch high-gloss black wheels. It retains the same engine and passenger capacity as the other trims.

Avenir

The Avenir adds premium amenities, including ventilated and massaging front seats, heated second-row outboard seats, a panoramic sunroof, a Bose Performance 16-speaker audio system and 22-inch wheels. Super Cruise and a Head-Up Display are also standard.
